1. What We Collect

When you register with Google Sign-In via Clerk.js, we store:

  • Your chosen username
  • Your email address (from Google, for account recovery only)
  • Your profile picture URL (from Google)
  • Game progress: completed missions, XP, stars earned, worlds joined

We do not collect passwords (authentication is handled entirely by Clerk/Google).

2. How We Use It

  • To save your game progress across devices
  • To display your username in multiplayer lobbies
  • To let you create and share custom worlds

3. Who Has Access

Your data is stored in our MongoDB database and is not shared with third parties. Clerk.js handles authentication — see Clerk's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You may delete your account at any time via the in-game profile menu. This removes your progress data and anonymizes your user record.

5. Age Requirement

You must be at least 16 years old to register an account. Players under 16 may play as guests without registering.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use localStorage to save game progress locally. Clerk.js uses cookies to manage your authentication session.
