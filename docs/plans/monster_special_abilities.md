# Monster Special Abilities

Ideas for making monsters more interesting and challenging.

## Projectile Attacks (Shield-Blockable)

- **Arrow Volley** — Shoots arrows that deal damage unless blocked by shield
- **Dark Fireballs** — Slow-moving projectiles that track the player
- **Poison Darts** — Deal damage over time unless blocked
- **Chain Lightning** — Bounces between players in multiplayer

## Status Effects (Shield-Preventable)

- **Freeze (Ice)** — Immobilizes player for 5 seconds, blocked by shield
- **Confusion** — Inverts controls for 3 seconds
- **Blindness** — Blackens screen edges, reducing visibility
- **Slow** — Reduces movement speed by 50% for 4 seconds
- **Silence** — Prevents ammo regeneration from quiz answers

## Area Effects

- **Fear Aura** — Pushes players away from the monster
- **Doubt Cloud** — Creates a zone where quiz time is reduced
- **Hellfire Patch** — Leaves burning ground that damages over time
- **Tar Pit** — Slows anyone walking through it

## Behavior Abilities

- **Teleport** — Vanishes and reappears near the player
- **Split** — Divides into two smaller demons when low health
- **Summon** — Calls weaker minion demons
- **Shield Itself** — Becomes immune briefly when attacking
- **Charge Attack** — Rushes toward player with wind-up animation
- **Burrow** — Goes underground, emerges unexpectedly

## Thematic by Demon Type

| Demon | Ability | Counter |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **Fear** | Fear Aura (pushback) | Move closer with shield |
| **Doubt** | Quiz time reduction | Shield prevents |
| **Condemnation** | Chains that bind | Shield blocks |
| **Temptation** | Lures player toward it | Shield resists pull |
| **Wrath** | Enrages at low HP | Kill quickly |
| **Pride** | Reflects bullets | Wait for cooldown |
| **Despair** | Drain HP over time | Shield blocks |
