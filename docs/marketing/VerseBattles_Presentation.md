# VerseBattles: Demon Chase
## A Fun Educational Tool for Meditating on God's Word

---

# The Problem

- **Bible retention is challenging** - Most Christians struggle to remember Scripture
- **Passive reading has limits** - Simply reading the Bible doesn't guarantee retention
- **Discipleship requires engagement** - Young believers need interactive ways to learn
- **Gaming is ubiquitous** - 3+ billion gamers worldwide spend billions of hours playing

---

# The Solution: VerseBattles

**An action-packed dungeon game where players fight demons by answering Bible verse quizzes**

🎮 **Action Combat** - Top-down dungeon crawler with demons representing spiritual struggles

📖 **Scripture Engagement** - Answer questions about real Bible verses to earn ammo and deal damage

🧠 **Memory Through Repetition** - Quiz modes reinforce verse retention through active recall

---

# Game Features

## 🎯 Multiple Quiz Modes

- **First Letter** - Type the first letter of the missing word
- **Missing Word** - Fill in the complete verse
- **Category Match** - Match verses to their books/categories
- **True/False** - Verify verse accuracy
- **Cloze** - Complete partial verses

---

## ⚔️ Combat System

- Fight demons representing spiritual battles
  - Fear, Doubt, Condemnation, Anger, Pride
- Answer correctly to shoot demons
- Earn XP and level up
- Collect power-ups: healing, shields, sword of the Spirit

---

## 🗺️ Campaign & Missions

- **3 Chapters** of progressive difficulty
- **6 Missions** with unique objectives
- **Overland Map** for campaign navigation
- Star ratings based on performance
- Difficulty settings: Easy, Normal, Hard

---

## 🎓 Educational Value

### Active Recall Method

### Contextual Learning
- Verses presented in gameplay context

- Start with easier quiz modes
- Advance to harder challenges as skills improve
