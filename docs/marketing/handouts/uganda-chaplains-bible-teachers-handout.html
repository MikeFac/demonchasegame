Chaplains & Bible Teachers

Help students return to Scripture with more energy and repetition.

VerseBattles can support teacher-led discipleship by turning Bible recall into a more active exercise. It is not meant to replace teaching, but to help students re-engage with the verse after the lesson or chapel message ends.
