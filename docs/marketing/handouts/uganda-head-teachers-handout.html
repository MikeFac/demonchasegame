Head Teachers

A cautious school pilot, not a heavy new technology programme.

For head teachers and school leaders, the value is simple: VerseBattles can be tested quickly on existing devices to see whether it genuinely strengthens Bible-memory engagement before the school commits broader time or attention.

No app-store rollout is required to begin a real test.
The same tool can be used across Bible class, clubs, and chapel follow-up.
If the pilot is weak, the school can stop early.
Leadership question: does this create enough repeatable Scripture engagement to justify school attention?

Why it may be worth testing

It gives Bible memory a more active format than routine recitation alone.
It can support Christian distinctiveness in student life.
Leadership can assess value under real school conditions first.

Where it may fit

Bible and RE periods
Scripture clubs or inter-house activities
Chaplaincy and chapel follow-up

Students can see clear activity paths from the start, which helps staff supervise the pilot more easily.
