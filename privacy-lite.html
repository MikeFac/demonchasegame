Privacy Policy — VerseBattles Lite

App: VerseBattles Lite (com.versebattles.lite)  ·  Effective: September 13, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 13, 2026

The short version

VerseBattles Lite is an offline Bible verse game. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything the game needs is bundled inside the app, and the app works fully with no internet connection.

  • No account or registration is required or offered.
  • No personal information is collected from anyone, of any age.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your own device.
  • No data is sent to us or to any third party.

1. Who we are

VerseBattles Lite is developed and published by Michael Fackerell ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the VerseBattles Lite Android application distributed on Google Play, and to no other product or service.

Privacy questions, requests, or complaints: michaelfackerell@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

We collect no personal data whatsoever. Specifically, the app does not collect, request, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Your location (precise or approximate)
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Your device identifiers, advertising ID, or IMEI
  • Your usage analytics, gameplay telemetry, or crash reports
  • Your files or any other app's data

There is no user account system, no login, no cloud save, and no synchronisation. Because no data is collected, we also have no data to sell, rent, trade, or disclose to advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game state locally on your own device so that your progress persists between sessions. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It includes:

  • Which verses you have studied and your learning progress
  • Your preferences, such as the Music On/Off toggle and display settings
  • Daily challenge progress and other in-game state

This data is stored in the app's private storage area. It is deleted automatically when you uninstall the app, or when you clear the app's data through your device's Settings → Apps → VerseBattles Lite → Storage → Clear data. We cannot retrieve it for you, because we never receive it.

4. Voice and text-to-speech

In Learn mode, the app can read Bible verses aloud. It does this using the text-to-speech engine already built into your device (for example the speech service provided by your phone's manufacturer or by Google). The verse text is passed directly to that on-device engine so it can be spoken; it is not sent to us or to any server we operate.

Your device's built-in speech engine is provided by your device manufacturer or operating system vendor and is governed by their privacy policy, not this one. Some devices download voice data through that vendor's own services. If no usable voice is installed, the app simply stays silent and continues to work normally — voice is always optional.

5. Internet access and the one external link

The app is designed and built to work with no network connection. It does not transmit any data about you or your device automatically, and it makes no background network requests.

The app does include a single optional, tap-only link: "Play the Full Version", in the main menu. If — and only if — you tap it, the app asks your device to open https://versebattles.com in your device's web browser. At that moment:

  • You leave the VerseBattles Lite app and enter your web browser.
  • Your browser will request that website, which means the website will receive your IP address and standard technical request information, as any website does.
  • That website is a separate service with its own privacy policy, which governs what it does with that information. We encourage you to read it before using that site.
  • Nothing about your in-app game progress is sent or shared when you tap the link.

The app declares the standard Android network permission because the WebView container needs it to open that single external web address. It is not used to collect or transmit your data. If you never tap "Play the Full Version", the app requires no network access at all.

6. Advertising and analytics

There is none, of any kind.

  • No advertising networks and no ad SDKs
  • No analytics or telemetry SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, or similar)
  • No tracking pixels, no cookies, and no advertising identifiers
  • No social media SDKs, no third-party login

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else — because we do not collect personal information at all. There is no account creation, no chat, no user-generated content, and no way for a child to submit personal information through the app. Parents and guardians may contact us at the address above with any question or concern.

8. Your privacy rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to its sale or sharing. Because we collect no personal data and share none, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, export, or delete, and we have never sold or shared personal data.

You remain in full control of the local game data on your device and can erase all of it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings. If you believe we hold data about you, contact us and we will investigate and respond.

9. Data security and retention

We hold no personal data, so there is no server-side database to breach and no retention period to describe. Local game data persists only on your device, only until you remove it, and is protected by your device's own security and encryption.
