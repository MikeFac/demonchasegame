Why Create an Account?

Registration is free and optional. You can play as a guest without an account.

💾 Save Your Progress — Your verse learning, levels, and achievements are saved to the cloud and sync across devices.
🌍 Create & Share Worlds — Design custom missions and share them with friends and the community.
🏆 Leaderboards — Compete with other players and track your learning journey.
🤝 Multiplayer — Join or host multiplayer games with other registered players.

Privacy Policy

VerseBattles is committed to protecting your privacy.

Age Requirement

You must be 16 years of age or older to create an account or use the registration features of VerseBattles. The game can be played without an account by users of any age under parental supervision.

Data Collection

  • Without an account: We use Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymous gameplay events (correct answers, levels completed), device type, browser, and general location (country/region). No personal information is collected.
  • With an account: If you register, we store your chosen username, email address (provided via Clerk authentication), gameplay progress, and any worlds you create. Authentication is handled securely by Clerk.
  • We do not collect or store passwords — authentication is handled entirely by Clerk's secure service.

Data Use

  • Analytics data helps us improve the game experience
  • Account data is used solely to provide game features (progress sync, world sharing, leaderboards)
  • We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising or user profiling
  • Data is not shared with third parties except Google (analytics) and Clerk (authentication)

Data Storage & Security

  • Account data is stored securely in MongoDB with encryption at rest
  • The game works offline — your local progress is stored in your browser and synced when online
  • We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information

Your Rights

  • You can delete your account at any time, which removes all your data from our servers
  • You can disable analytics collection in your browser settings
  • You can request a copy of your data by contacting us
  • Registration is entirely optional — you can play the full game without an account

Children's Privacy

VerseBattles does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we discover that a child under 16 has created an account, we will promptly delete it. Parents and guardians can contact us to request removal of their child's data.
