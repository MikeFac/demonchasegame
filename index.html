Privacy Policy

VerseBattles does not collect or sell personal information.

Data Collection

  • We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how players use the game
  • Analytics collects: gameplay events (correct answers, levels completed, monsters defeated), device type, browser, and general location (country/region)
  • No personal information such as names, emails, or personally identifiable information are collected
  • Google Analytics data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy

Data Use

  • Analytics data helps us improve the game and measure how players discover and use VerseBattles
  • We do not share data with third parties except Google
  • Data is not used for targeted advertising or user profiling

Your Rights

  • You can disable analytics collection in your browser settings
  • We do not use tracking cookies (only session cookies for gameplay)
  • Your gameplay data is not saved or associated with any account

Contact

Questions about our privacy practices? Email us at michaelfackerell@gmail.com
