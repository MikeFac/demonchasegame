# Default player rigging reference

`player-default-a-pose.png` was generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool
for use as a Tripo P1 automatic-rigging reference.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: rigging reference for a mobile 3D web-game player character

Create a clean, production-ready reference image of the game's default human
player as a young adult male guardian/adventurer, designed specifically for
automatic biped rigging. Use standard realistic human proportions, a friendly
but determined face, short neat blond hair, warm medium-light skin, a simple
royal-blue knee-length tunic over fitted blue trousers, subtle gold edging and
small gold belt details, and dark brown ankle boots. The design should feel
appealing and heroic but modest, practical, and suitable for a broad family
audience.

Show the exact full body, centered, in a straight-on front view and symmetrical
neutral A-pose: arms 40 degrees away from the torso, elbows straight, palms
forward, fingers relaxed and visibly separated, legs slightly apart, knees
straight, and feet flat and shoulder-width. Use a torso-height orthographic
character-sheet camera with no perspective distortion. Keep the entire head,
fingertips, and boots visible with generous padding.

Use a plain light-gray studio background and polished realistic-stylized 3D
game-character rendering with clean mobile-game forms and readable anatomy.
Use even studio lighting intended to reveal anatomy and clothing boundaries.

Require clear gaps between arms and torso and between legs. Head, neck,
shoulders, wrists, elbows, groin, knees, and ankles must be unambiguous. One
character only, bilateral symmetry, and no occlusion.

Avoid swords, weapons, shields, capes, robe, coat tails, armor plates, helmets,
props, wings, tails, scenery, floor plane, floor or cast shadows, dramatic pose,
bent joints, crossed limbs, foreshortening, text, logos, watermarks, extra
objects, or cropped anatomy.
